⏱️ How Long Does It Take to Read?

At an average reading speed of 250 words per minute and roughly 1,500 words per chapter:

  • Per chapter: ~6 minutes
  • First 100 chapters: ~10 hours
  • All 2,400+ chapters: ~240 hours (10 full days of non-stop reading)

Most readers take 2–4 months to fully catch up, reading a few hours per day.
